Any data you create or store within StarterSeeds remains locally on your iPhone. This includes:

This data is never transmitted to me or any third party.

## Apple Analytics

If you have opted in to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings (*Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share iPhone & Watch Analytics*), Apple may provide me with anonymous, aggregated crash reports and basic usage statistics through Xcode.

This data:

- Is entirely controlled by your iOS settings
- Is anonymous and cannot be linked to you personally
- Helps me identify and fix technical issues
- Is only available if you explicitly enable it in your device settings

I do not request, collect, or access this data beyond what Apple automatically provides to developers who have apps in the App Store. Where this anonymous data reaches me, the legal basis is my legitimate interest (Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR) in maintaining a stable, bug-free app. Apple acts as an independent controller for this data; see Apple's own privacy policy for details on how Apple processes it.

## Third-Party Services
