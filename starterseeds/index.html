About

StarterSeeds is an iOS game launcher designed for people who want quick access to their mobile games. Pin your favourites to the home screen, browse your complete game library, and even view your photo gallery with a controller.

The app is built with privacy in mind: no analytics SDKs, no trackers, no third-party services. All your data stays on your device.

To use StarterSeeds as your default launcher with a controller, go to Settings > General > Game Controller > Controller Launcher.

Features

  • Game Launcher: Start all your iOS games from a single app.
  • Favorites: Pin the games you play most to the home screen.
  • Full Library: Scroll through every game installed on your device.
  • Tag Management (Pro): Create and assign tags to organize your library.
  • Library Search: Quickly find the game you are looking for.
  • Gamepad Navigation: Navigate the toolbar, grids, filters, and popovers with a controller.
  • Photo Gallery: Browse your photos in the app, optimized for controller use.
  • Privacy First: No data collection, no third-party trackers.
